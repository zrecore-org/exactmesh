# Guarantees and limits

What this repository claims, what it does not, and how each claim was tested. Numbers are from the research phase (Apple M1 unless stated) and are reproduced by the scripts in `ci/`
(see `REPRODUCE.md`). "Row" means one pair of meshes and one operation (union, intersection or difference).

## What is exact

* **Validation.** Zero-area triangles, orientation (sign of the exact volume), duplicate triangles, open and non-manifold edges (as returned and after welding vertices with exactly equal coordinates) are decided with exact
  integer arithmetic (`harness/exact.h`, Boost.Multiprecision), behind a double-precision filter whose bound is conservative; an inconclusive filter falls through to the exact test.
* **Scaling.** The pipeline multiplies both operands by one power of two so that every coordinate is below 2^18 in magnitude. This is lossless; it is refused (hard limit, "underflow") if a nonzero coordinate would become subnormal or inexact.
* **Re-centring.** Both operands are translated by one vector `c` (a multiple of 2^e, e = lowest set bit of all scaled coordinates) **only if every `x - c` is exactly representable** (checked per coordinate with TwoSum). Otherwise `c = 0` is used when
  `|bbox centre| <= 2^12 x (largest bbox side)`.
* **Boolean predicates.** Geogram evaluates its geometric predicates exactly (expansion arithmetic, with `-frounding-math -ffp-contract=off`); the two patches change only how components are classified.
* **Judging.** Volumes in the tests are compared as exact dyadic rationals, never with a tolerance, except where a row is marked "close" in the reports (curved results have no exact expected volume and are judged by exact point-in-solid tests on 5,000 sample points).

## What is rounded

* Geogram computes the intersection vertices as exact rational points and **rounds them to binary64** in the working frame. The result is therefore the exact Boolean of the inputs up to vertex rounding, not the exact result.
* **Translate-back.** The result is translated back by `c` (one IEEE addition per coordinate) and scaled back by 2^s (exact). Measured maximum error of the translate-back addition: **0.5 ulp** of the original-frame value
  (42,890 of 1,320,765 result coordinates were rounded over 9,204 passing rows). Geogram's own rounding in the working frame is not included in that figure.

## What is refused, and why

| Refusal | Why (hard limit) |
|---|---|
| open surface, non-manifold edge, duplicate or inverted-twin triangle, zero-area triangle, inverted orientation, empty mesh, non-finite coordinate; since V2 also `V1-NESTED-SAME-ORIENTATION`, `V1-NEGATIVE-OUTSIDE`, `V1-ZERO-VOLUME-COMPONENT`, `V1-SURFACES-SHARED`, `V1-NESTING-INCONSISTENT`, and `V1-CAVITY-NOT-ALLOWED` with the option `--no-cavities` | The pipeline does not repair input; an invalid solid has no well-defined Boolean. Exit status 3, reason on stderr. |
| `hard limit (far from the origin)` | The pair cannot be re-centred exactly and `|bbox centre| / largest side > 2^12`: Geogram's own signed-volume classification cancels catastrophically on absolute coordinates far from the origin (defect B below). 222 of 2,412 fresh-family rows (class `cfar`) are refused here; 0 of them passed by accident. |
| `hard limit (underflow)` | Exact scaling by 2^-s would turn a nonzero coordinate into a subnormal or inexact value (6 rows: coordinates spanning 2^1000 down to 2^-100). |
| `geogram threw` | Geogram raised an exception (assert mode). Counted as a refusal; none occurred in the recorded evaluation. |

Not refused although it is not valid input: **self-intersecting meshes** (the validator does not check for them; the one such row in the corpus passed through).

## Where it was tested

* **Corpora (all synthetic, exact expectations, generated byte-reproducibly; `corpus/`)**: G1 (93 rows: boxes, spheres, tori, malformed), the offset family (1,533 rows, offsets 2^0 to 2^50), the thin family (513 rows, features down to 2^-40), touch sets 3 and 5 (4,902 rows of
  vertex/edge/face contact), and the fresh families (2,412 rows: nested containers 2^15 to 2^40, near and far curved meshes, underflow). Total 9,453 rows.
* **Platforms**: Apple M1 (arm64, Apple clang 21, libc++); Linux x86_64 and aarch64 (g++ 13.3, libstdc++) in the CI of the research branch: identical verdicts on all three (`e2edd6f7...`), identical bytes between the two Linux architectures, different bytes from the M1
  (libc++ vs libstdc++; every row correct on all three). This repository's own CI has not yet run on GitHub; its jobs were run by hand in Ubuntu 24.04 containers (aarch64 natively, x86_64 under emulation) with the same result: identical verdicts and identical output bytes on both (`REPRODUCE.md`).
* **GPU**: soft binary64 add/mul bit-identical to hardware doubles (random and adversarial, with planted mutants caught); the orient3d filter shows 0 wrong definite signs; the LBVH pair lists equal the CPU reference on Apple M1 (MoltenVK), NVIDIA Tesla T4, and lavapipe (x86_64, aarch64),
  with identical pair-list hashes across platforms.

## What is NOT tested

* **Real meshes beyond one subset.** Only a Thingi10K subset was run (see "Real-mesh evidence"); models above 200k triangles, non-manifold or self-intersecting inputs, scans, and any platform other than the M1 were not. Synthetic corpora and one subset can miss whole classes of input.
* **Windows**, MSVC, and every standard library or compiler other than Apple clang 21 / libc++ and g++ 13.3 / libstdc++ (Geogram has a known dependence on memory layout, see defect 2).
* **Non-contact families**: the corpora concentrate on contact, offset, thin and nested cases. Curved-curved contact has no unpatched failure to repair and a thin sample; spiky near-coincident geometry fails regardless of coordinate magnitude (76 of 186 development runs
  of one spiky shape failed, 17 of them at small coordinates; a Geogram behaviour that the pipeline does not address and the acceptance families exclude by using smooth shapes).
* **GPU hardware** other than Apple M1, NVIDIA T4 and lavapipe; performance claims for GPU are not made here (see `RESULTS.md` for what was measured and its caveats).
* Non-manifold repair, self-intersection handling, meshes above a few hundred thousand triangles, multi-threaded Geogram determinism beyond the single-thread (`GEOGRAM_RUNNER_THREADS=1`) runs used for scoring.

## V1: nesting and orientation (validator library; 2026-11-14)

* **Rule.** Per connected component (shared edges after welding by equal coordinates) the validator computes the exact signed volume and the exact containment of components (exact ray-parity point-in-solid on one vertex of the inner component that is not on the other surface; bounding-box prefilter). Signs must alternate with nesting depth (solid +, cavity -, island +, ...); a component with no container must be positive; a component of zero volume is refused. Reason codes: `V1-ZERO-VOLUME-COMPONENT`, `V1-NEGATIVE-OUTSIDE`, `V1-NESTED-SAME-ORIENTATION`, `V1-SURFACES-SHARED`, `V1-NESTING-INCONSISTENT`, and (strict mode, `allowCavities=false`) `V1-CAVITY-NOT-ALLOWED`.
* **A cavity is accepted by default** (an inverted shell strictly inside a positive one is a hollow solid; the validator cannot know whether the caller wanted it) and counted in the report; only strict mode refuses it. This deliberately differs from "refuse all three known-limit inputs": the nested inverted shell is refused only in strict mode.
* **Rule differs from "strictly inside exactly one positive component".** A cavity inside an island inside a cavity is inside two positive components; the alternating-sign rule accepts it.
* **Not detected:** crossing component surfaces and coplanar or touching component surfaces (still no self-intersection check; the nesting decision is exact only for components whose surfaces do not cross; pinned by tests that assert the current behaviour). If every vertex of a component lies on another component's surface the input is refused as undecidable.
* **Cost:** component labelling for every input, plus O(C^2) bounding-box tests and exact point-in-solid for nested candidates when there are C > 1 components (4,800 disjoint boxes: 0.009 s frozen validator, 0.069 s with V1 on this machine; a single component adds only the labelling). Very large component counts (10^5 and more) would be quadratic. Not measured on the real-mesh set (not available to this run).
* **Pipeline CLI (V2).** `geogram_fidelity2 [--no-cavities] boolean <op> A B out` runs the frozen validator, then V1, on A and on B; a refusal is `REFUSED: invalid input A|B: <reason>`, exit 3. Cavities are accepted by default (a hollow solid is a legitimate operand) and refused only with `--no-cavities`. **Cavity operands are not guaranteed correct:** the sample of 256 cavity pairs run through the CLI had failures and Geogram exceptions in the one-ulp-gap family and hard-limit refusals of far-offset pairs (`docs/RESULTS.md`, section V2); those are behaviours of the Boolean engine on valid inputs, identical before V2.
* **Evidence:** `docs/RESULTS.md`, sections V1 and V2.

## Real-mesh evidence (MP26, Thingi10K subset, Apple M1)

420 pairs from 560 models (closed, manifold, no self-intersections, up to 200k triangles; selection frozen and hashed before any run; `research/realmesh/`, recorded results by model id only, no model file is in this repository and each model has its own licence).
* **407 clean pairs: 0 wrong results** (405 correct, 2 refused, per operation); plain patched Geogram 407/0/0 and Manifold 407/0/0 on the same pairs. Over all 420 pairs the pipeline gave 410 correct, 0 wrong, 10 refused; Manifold gave 12/5/12 wrong (union/intersection/difference) on the 13 ambiguous pairs.
* **Every refusal was an input defect**, all by the exact validator and none a hard limit: 7 whole-model inversions and 2 exact zero-area triangles that the dataset's own metadata does not flag, plus 1 artefact of the pair builder.
* **Validator gap (found here; closed by V1 in the validator library and wired into the pipeline CLI by V2).** At MP26 the validator checked only the sign of the *total* exact volume, so five accepted pairs (p001, p152, p235, p302, p304) had nested same-orientation shells or negative-volume components and their "correct" was established only on the unambiguous samples. V1 (`lib/geogram/v1/validator_v1.h`, section below) now refuses those classes; since V2 the pipeline CLI `geogram_fidelity2` calls V1 as well, so nested same-orientation shells and negative-volume components are refused with their V1 reason code (the real-mesh set was NOT re-run, so which of those five pairs are now refused is not measured). The old `[known-limit]` test was converted to an ordinary passing test; its original assertions (the three inputs are accepted) survive as "historical: the frozen validator ... accepts" and still hold for `mp21b::validate` alone (the pipeline CLI no longer behaves like that).
* Residual of the inclusion-exclusion volume identity on clean pairs: median 4e-18, max 4.5e-15. Pipeline time equals plain Geogram (+2%); Manifold is about 8x faster at the median.

## The recorded bar that is not met

Registered bar MP24b: "0 FAIL and only hard-limit refusals". Result: **NOT MET** by 3 FAIL rows, all from the pair `f2_cfarrc_L3r_j40_shift` (full-mantissa sphere at offset 2^40; all three operations). The diagnosis (exact arithmetic; `docs/RESULTS.md`, "MP24b: diagnosis")
finds the output correct up to vertex rounding: the scoring rule flags a collapsed triangle that vertex rounding creates. The bar was not edited. Bars 1 to 4 of MP24 are met under the amended pipeline.

## The three Geogram defect groups found, and the patches

All three are defects of Geogram v1.10.1 (commit `bbcacba146b357926b5524111f56cc70c42182a7`) found by this project's corpora; none has been reported upstream.

1. **Vertex-contact classification (patch `geogram_mp20.patch`).** Components that touch other components only at a vertex are classified by one ray from the component's start vertex; when that vertex is the shared contact vertex, the tie is broken by `orient_3d_SOS` using **pointer order**, not geometry,
   so e.g. the union of two boxes sharing one corner returns one box. The patch starts the ray from an exact facet centroid for components whose start vertex is shared. Unpatched failures: 360 of 3,150 rows on the acceptance set. Wrapping the call (vertex-order voting) was tried and failed (21 silent failures, 23.2% refusals).
2. **Ray through a contact vertex, Linux only (patch `geogram_mp22.patch`, applied after MP20).** For an *unpatched* component whose +z ray passes exactly through another body's contact vertex and lies in a facet plane there, `classify()` runs after the SOS mode is reset to address order, so the tie is again broken by
   pointer order, which differs between libstdc++ and libc++ (the M1 does not show it). The patch uses only exact `orient_3d`, skips facets by bounding box, and tilts the ray through up to 8 fixed offsets when a facet is hit degenerately.
3. **Coordinate-magnitude defects (not patched; handled by the pipeline).** (A) a hard-coded `1e6` in the inclusion-bit ray (`q1.z + 1e6`, five places) makes a component more than 1e6 from its enclosing surface classify wrongly (a box nested in a cube of half-width >= 2^20 gives an empty intersection);
   (B) `classify()` picks the external chart with a plain-double signed-volume sum on absolute coordinates, which cancels catastrophically far from the origin (overlapping boxes at offset >= 2^26 return a correct shape with inverted orientation). Exact scaling below 2^18 removes (A); exact re-centring removes (B); where re-centring is impossible the pipeline refuses (see above).
